About us

This project was developed as part of SEAS 2026 — Summer in Engineering & Applied Science, a prestigious summer research program for students passionate about applying technology to solve real-world challenges. We built this intelligent travel recommendation platform with the goal of making it easier for everyone to discover and experience the rich culture, history, and natural beauty of Central Vietnam.
